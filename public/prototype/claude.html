Our process

From billet to finished part

Every stage is controlled in-house, so each component meets your drawing.

01

Raw Material

Bars from approved mills (ISMT, JSW, Gerdau). Chemical, UT & colour-code identification.

02

Die Making

In-house die shop with VMC. DIN-2714 / H11 / H13 dies, layout inspected on Plaster of Paris.

03

Blank Cutting

Circular saws with auto loader, band saws and hot shearing on long bars.

04

Heating

Induction billet heaters with temperature accept/reject, plus an oil-fired furnace.

05

Forging

Drawing down, upsetting, piercing, cup forming and ring rolling in one heat cycle.

06

Heat Treatment

Bell-type normalizing & spheroidized annealing with live i-Tool cycle tracking.

07

Shot Blasting

Tumbler-type descaling with 0.45 / 0.55 mm shot and 100% fettling.

08

Inspection

Visual, Brinell hardness, MPI crack detection and pre-dispatch inspection reports.
