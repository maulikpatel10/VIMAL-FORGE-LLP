Material Metallurgy

Certified Steel Grades & Capabilities

We audit raw alloy steel bars at incoming reception. Our metallurgical laboratory ensures exact carbon levels and clean grain boundaries before shearing.

SAE 52100 / 100Cr6 Chrome Bearing Steel

Known for exceptional hardness, wear resistance, and high contact fatigue strength. It is the global standard for outer/inner rings and rollers in anti-friction bearings.

  • Application: Deep groove bearings, taper roller races
  • Curing: Spheroidize Annealed for optimal machineability
  • Hardness Limit: 58 - 62 HRC after heat treatment
  • Metallurgical Audit: Strict micro-inclusion rating

20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 8620, EN19 & EN24

Premium case-hardening and alloy steel grades offering tough core properties and surface wear immunity after carburizing or induction hardening.

  • Application: Transmission gears, axles, heavy pins
  • Feature: High impact resistance under extreme torque
  • Heat Treatment: Carburizing / Hardening & Tempering
  • Grain Size: ASTM 5-8 fine grain structure

EN8, C45, C20 & Mild Steels

Excellent tensile strength and machining adaptability. Used for structural rings, spacers, and custom flanges not subject to extreme bearing friction.

  • Application: Spacers, hydraulic cylinder bushes, structural brackets
  • Treatment: Supplied in Normalized or As-Forged state
  • Tolerances: Clean structural density with zero internal voids
  • Cost Benefit: High yield cost-effective industrial steel

SS 304, SS 316 & SS 410

High-integrity corrosion resistant and chemical process steel. Forged under strictly monitored temperatures to prevent carbide precipitation.

  • Application: Pipe fittings, marine rings, chemical valves
  • Feature: Immunity to oxidation and acidic/salty environments
  • Testing: Intergranular Corrosion Test (IGC) compliant
  • Grade standard: ASTM A182 / EN 10222-5
OEM Development Journey

Custom Component Development

How we take a client design sheet and deliver finished components within tight project timelines.

01

Engineering Review & Design

Submission of client 2D drawing or 3D CAD/STEP model. Our design office reviews tolerances and material suitability.

02

DEFORM-3D Simulation

Virtual forging simulation models metal flow, grain alignment, and temperature distributions to eliminate cracking risks.

03

In-House Tooling (15 Days)

Die-manufacturing using high-speed vertical machining centers (VMC). Prototyping dies are checked via coordinates measuring.

04

Forging & Heat Treatment

Components undergo hot closed die forging or radial-axial ring rolling. Heat treatments (Normalizing, Spheroidizing) are applied.

05

Micron Machining & Inspection

Vimal's CNC lathes turn components within 15-micron tolerances. Chemical and non-destructive testing (NDT) audits follow.
