Quality Control

Metallurgical Inspection

Quality control is integrated into every step of the forging, rolling, and machining processes at Vimal Forge LLP. We check raw metal bars chemical composition before cutting, check temperatures during hot forging, check structure after annealing, and check tolerances using air-gauging machines.

Inspection systems

Metrology & Laboratory Equipment

🔬

Spectro Analysis

GNR Italy Optical Emission Spectrometer checks chemical elements in incoming steel billets to verify chromium, carbon, and nickel percentages.

🧲

Magnetic Particle (MPI)

Mayuresh wet magnetic particle crack checking identifies surface seams and hairline micro-cracks on 100% of finished bearing rings.

🔊

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Sub-surface non-destructive checking detects internal voids, blowholes, or micro-porosities inside forged preform cores.

📐

Metrology Gauging

Digital micrometers, dial air-gauging systems, and profile projectors verify finish turned dimensions within strict micron limits.

Verification Trail

Material Traceability Journey

We maintain a strict traceability trail to guarantee that every single component dispatched to an OEM can be tracked back to its original mill heat source.

1

Heat Code Color Coding

Incoming steel bars are assigned a unique color code matching the steel mill batch number on arrival at Shapar warehouse.

2

Hot Forging Stamping

During hammer drop or CNC rolling forming, a physical steel indent stamp is applied to permanently embed the code.

3

Laser Heat Marking

Finished machined rings undergo permanent laser engraving of the specific heat number on non-bearing landing faces.

4

MTR Certificate Release

Heat-wise chemical reports conforming to EN 10204 3.1 and inspection logs are attached with the export cargo.

QA Documentation Package

Every dispatch shipment is accompanied by a full quality assurance dossier containing:

  • Material Test Report (MTR): Heat-wise reports conforming to EN 10204 3.1.
  • Dimensional Layout Logs: Showing metrology inspection limits against target values.
  • Annealing Furnace Charts: Pyrometer heat curves indicating slow cooling curves.
  • NDT Test Certificates: Validations for Ultrasonic (UT) and Magnetic Particle (MPI) audits.
  • VCI Rust Protection Logs: Checks verifying anti-rust oil density parameters.
