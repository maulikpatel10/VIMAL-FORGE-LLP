Billet-to-Component Lifecycle

Physical Progression Journey

See how we transform raw steel into high-precision finished components using our integrated operations.

1. Raw Material

Certified alloy steel bars sourced from approved prime mills, color coded and chemical spectro tested.

2. Forged Component

Hot forged drop parts or CNC rolled rings with seamless grain layout orientation for high stress loads.

3. Machined Component

Finish-turned on CNC lathes and slotted on vertical VMCs. Critical seating dimensions ground within +/-10μm.

4. Finished Product

Laser heat-coded, NDT inspected, oil dipped, and sealed inside VCI moisture-barrier bags on wood pallets.

Step-by-Step Production Flow

10-Step Value Chain

01

Raw Material Sourcing & Spectro Check

Procured only from approved prime steel mills. Billets are chemical spectro checked upon arrival to match Mill Test Certificates (MTC) exactly.

02

Billet Shearing & Cutting

Billets are cut to calculated weights via circular and band saws. Consistent weights minimize material flash loss and maximize yield values.

03

Induction Billet Heating

Induction heating maintains a uniform temperature throughout the steel preforms, preventing scale formation and internal thermal micro-cracks.

04

Closed Die Forging

Billets are drop-forged inside detailed tool rooms die cavities. Establishes structural grain flows required for high stress applications.

05

CNC Ring Rolling

Preforms are punched and expanded on automatic radial-axial CNC ring rolling mills to exact outer diameters and wall thicknesses.

06

Controlled Heat Treatment

Uniform heating and slow cooling curves (spheroidized annealing) eliminate internal stress and optimize machinability limits.

07

Precision Machining (CNC & VMC)

Finish turning on turning centers, and milling slot alignments on VMCs. Bearings seating faces ground within 10 microns limit.

08

Quality Inspection & NDT Testing

Hairline cracks checks using wet MPI, and sub-surface validation using Ultrasonic waves. Unique heat numbers laser marks components.

09

Rust Prevention & VCI Packaging

Dipped inside premium anti-rust oil, bagged with Volatile Corrosion Inhibitors (VCI), and packed onto heat-treated pallets.

10

Logistics & Dispatch

Trucked directly to Gujarat ports (Mundra/Pipavav) and loaded into cargo ships along with Mill Test Certs (MTR) and dimensional layouts.
