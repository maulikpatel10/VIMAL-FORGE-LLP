OEM Case Studies

Real-world examples demonstrating how our technical team optimizes production flow to drive material yield savings and precision quality.

Bearing Industry

Optimizing Material Yield for Tapered Outer Races

Problem:

A European bearing OEM was sourcing outer rings from a machine shop that cut them from solid bar stock, resulting in a high steel waste percentage (45% scrap rate) and high unit costs.

Solution:

We transitioned the component development to our CNC radial-axial ring rolling line. The preform is punched out and rolled, eliminating the need to bore out the center material.

Manufacturing Approach:

Forged in chrome bearing steel SAE 52100, spheroidize annealed in controlled furnaces, and rough-turned to 0.5mm grinding allowance in-house.

Results & Cost Savings:

Reduced raw material steel waste from 45% down to 12%, achieving an overall unit cost saving of 22% for the customer.

Automotive Industry

Eliminating Gear Blank Distortion During Carburization

Problem:

An Indian tractor manufacturer was experiencing tooth distortion and cracking issues on transmission gear blanks after final gas carburizing, leading to assembly-line rejects.

Solution:

Our metallurgy team determined the forging grain structure was inconsistent. We redesigned the closed-die hammer tools and introduced isothermal annealing heat treatment.

Manufacturing Approach:

Forged in 20MnCr5 steel. Implemented computerized die simulation to verify metal flow, followed by precise isothermal furnace cooling cycles.

Results & Cost Savings:

Isothermal structure eliminated post-heat treatment distortion. Assembly-line reject rate dropped from 3.2% to absolute 0%.

Hydraulic Industry

Near-Net Forging of Earth-Moving Cylinder Sleeves

Problem:

A heavy machinery vendor needed custom cylinder sleeves. The thick walls required long CNC boring times, creating supply bottlenecks for their assembly plant.

Solution:

We developed an upset closed-die forging process that punches out the internal bore shape during the hot forging stage itself, leaving a near-net component.

Manufacturing Approach:

Forged in EN19 high-tensile alloy steel. Machined on our high-power CNC lathes with automatic boring bars to final dimension tolerances (+/- 15 microns).

Results & Cost Savings:

Reduced OEM customer's turning/boring machining cycle times by 60%, speeding up their supply line by 3x.
