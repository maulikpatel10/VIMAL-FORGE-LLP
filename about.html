The Story of Vimal Forge LLP

Vimal Forge LLP is a leading integrated forging, ring rolling, and precision machining manufacturer based in Rajkot, India. Our group operates multiple state-of-the-art units, combining unified operations under Vimal Steel Forge and Vimal Forge LLP.

Since 2009, we have established a reputation as a trusted manufacturing partner for global OEMs in bearing, automotive, hydraulic, oil & gas, agriculture, and power transmission industries. By handling tooling design, hot forging, heat treatment, high-precision machining, and lab inspections fully in-house, we guarantee zero-defect compliance.

Evolution

Our Growth Timeline

2009

Vimal Steel Forge Founded

Established our first unit in Shapar-Veraval (Rajkot) to manufacture forged rings, seamless rolled rings, and bearing races. Installed early hammer lines and ring rolling mills to supply local bearing manufacturers.

2015

Expansion of Ring Rolling Operations

Upgraded to high-speed CNC radial-axial ring rolling machinery. Expanded our seamless rolled ring diameter capability up to 500mm and established heat treatment normalization lines for bearing races.

2022

Vimal Forge LLP Established

Built a new advanced forging shop in Pipadiya-Gondal. Integrated heavy closed-die hammers and friction screw presses with high-precision CNC lathe lines to supply fully-machined OEM components.

2024

Manufacturing Capacity Expansion

Expanded floor space at Unit 2, adding a 400-ton friction screw press line. Upgraded metrology labs with Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM) and spectrometer instrumentation for testing alloy compositions.

2026

Modern Integrated Manufacturing Group

Unified Vimal Steel Forge and Vimal Forge LLP into a single comprehensive sourcing platform. Fully prepared to scale finished bearing races and precision forgings to leading OEMs globally with 100% heat-wise traceability.
