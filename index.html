10-Step Value Chain

How We Manufacture Precision

STAGE 01
01

Raw Material Selection & Spectro Testing

Procuring high-quality certified billets from approved steel mills (SAE52100, Carbon & Alloy steels) and running extensive spectro-chemical testing to verify composition before sawing.

02

Precision Billets Shearing & Cutting

High-speed band sawing machines shear billets into exact calculated weights with zero weight tolerance to minimize raw material waste during forging.

03

Controlled Induction Bar Heating

Cut billets pass through induction furnaces, heating steel blanks uniformly to the exact plastic-deformation temperature under automatic temperature monitoring.

04

Pneumatic Hammer & Press Forging

Heated billets are compressed under high-impact forging hammers, aligning the grain flow to achieve superior fatigue and impact strength.

05

Seamless Ring Rolling Process

Blanks are punched and rolled on our high-speed radial-axial CNC ring rolling machines to generate seamless profiles with exact circular geometries.

06

Isothermal Annealing Heat Treatment

Rolled rings are heat-treated in continuous annealing furnaces to normalize internal stresses, refine grains, and achieve optimal machining hardness.

07

CNC Rough Machining

Modern CNC lathes perform rough turning to clear decarb layers and form dimensions close to the drawing specifications.

08

Precision CNC Finish Turning

High-precision turning centers finish all race tracks, faces, and grooves to meet micron-level tolerances required by industrial OEMs.

09

Strict QA Dimensional & Hardness Audit

In-house quality labs test micro-hardness, ovality, concentricity, and utilize CMM systems to audit every part before release.

10

Rust Preventive Coating & Safe Sea Dispatch

Finished parts receive anti-rust oil coatings and are safely packed in heavy-duty wooden boxes for sea-worthy transit.
